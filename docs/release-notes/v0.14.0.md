# v0.14.0

## Overview

A webmail release for wider screens. The desktop layout now uses the phone's navigation: one bar along the top of each page and one navigation, which is pinned open beside the page on a wider screen (over 760px). Its 📌 button unpins it, so that ☰ opens it as a drawer over the page, as on a phone. The desktop header, its ⋮ menu and the side folder list are gone. The phone layout is unchanged. The backend and the relay bridge are unchanged.

## Highlights

For people using the webmail on a wider screen:

- **One navigation**, pinned beside the page by default. It holds:
  - with a mailbox open, the mailboxes with their unread counts (a cut-off address shows in full on hover), and Inbox, Sent, Spam, Trash and Calendar;
  - Blocked senders and Download a copy with a mailbox open, and Admin for controllers;
  - the storage used, Reload with the release, and Sign out.
- **📌 pins and unpins it.** This browser keeps the choice. Pinned, ☰ folds it away and back. Unpinned, ☰ opens it as a drawer over the page, which Escape, a click beside it, or a choice closes.
- **One bar along the top of each page:** ☰, the page's title over the mailbox address, and the page's own actions. In Inbox, Spam, Trash and Sent the bar spans the list and the open message:
  - in Inbox, Spam and Trash, the search field, with ✕ to clear a search, and a ⋮ with Refresh, Select all, Blocked senders in Spam, and, where offered, Empty;
  - in Sent, a ⋮ with Select all and, where offered, Empty Sent.
- **Ticking a message's box** turns the bar into the selection bar: ☰, ✕ to untick them all, the number selected, every action for the folder, and a ⋮ with Select all. The box keeps the focus, for ticking the next one. The search field returns once nothing is ticked.
- **Refresh** is in the list's ⋮.
- **Compose floats** at the bottom right, as on a phone, and hides while a message is being written.
- The navigation is 220px wide, so a message laid out 600px wide still fits the reader at a window width of 1280px.

At phone width nothing changes, except that the list's bar is drawn the same way as at a wider width.

For maintainers:

- One navigation in `App.tsx`, shown docked in a `<nav>` or as the drawer (`Drawer.tsx`). The pin is kept in the browser's storage as `navigation:pinned`.
- Every page's bar (`AppBar.tsx`) is drawn into one place above the page, `.app-head`. `AppBar` keeps ☰ before a mode's own button at a wider width, and puts the button in ☰'s place at phone width.
- `useSelectMode` (`Bulk.tsx`) decides whether the selection bar shows: in selection mode at phone width, or while a box is ticked at a wider width. It also gives the list's ⋮ its Select messages or Select all.
- The header, its menu and the side list are removed, with their styles.
- Tests: the Playwright tests use the navigation and the bar's ⋮ in place of the header menu. New tests cover:
  - the pinned navigation and ☰ folding it;
  - unpinning, the drawer at a wider width, and the choice kept after a reload;
  - the list's bar spanning the reader;
  - the selection bar at a wider width, ☰ included;
  - the search field in the bar;
  - the address on hover;
  - the focus as the navigation and the bars change.

## Upgrade notes

**Deploy only the webmail.** The backend is unchanged: this release's wasm is v0.12.2's, sha256 `462e5902a9147abb938e860f8db9c64a0df628a0684588d9311c4eb032f4798e`. So do not stop or upgrade the backend, and take no snapshot. The relay bridge is unchanged.

1. Pin v0.14.0 in the deployment repository.
2. Deploy the webmail, rebuilt with the deployment's brand. The release's unbranded `dist` hash is `DIST_HASH`; the branded output differs from it.
3. Run the deployment's checks, with the wasm sha256 above.
4. Reload the webmail in each open browser. A browser may keep the old page until it is reloaded; the old page has Reload in its header's ⋮ menu on a wider screen, and in its ☰ drawer on a phone.

This holds for a deployment on v0.13.0 or v0.12.2. A deployment on an earlier release first upgrades its backend to v0.12.2 by the path v0.12.2's upgrade notes give, which installs no release they skip; a backend before v0.5.0 first upgrades as v0.5.0's upgrade notes say. It also makes the checks and the changes to `webmail/public/.ic-assets.json5` that the upgrade notes of the releases in between name. Then it deploys this webmail.

**Rolling back** means deploying v0.13.0's webmail again.

## Webmail and settings changes

- At a wider width: the pinned navigation, the bar along the top, the selection bar, the search field in the bar, and the floating Compose, as above.
- The browser keeps one new setting, `navigation:pinned`.
- `webmail/public/.ic-assets.json5`, the `brand.mjs` options and the brand format are unchanged.

## Known issues
