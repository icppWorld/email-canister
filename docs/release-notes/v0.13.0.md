# v0.13.0

## Overview

A webmail release for phones. At phone width (760px and less), the navigation used to take about half the screen. It now takes one bar along the top, and the rest moves into a ☰ drawer and ⋮ menus, so the mail starts near the top. Compose floats at the bottom right. The desktop layout is unchanged. The backend and the relay bridge are unchanged.

## Highlights

For people using the webmail on a phone:

- **One bar along the top.** It holds ☰, the page's title with the mailbox address under it, and the page's own actions:
  - in Inbox, Spam and Trash, 🔍 and a ⋮ with Refresh, Select messages and, where offered, Empty;
  - in Sent, a ⋮ with Select messages and, where offered, Empty Sent;
  - Calendar, Blocked senders and Admin show their title.
- **The ☰ drawer** holds:
  - with a mailbox open, the mailboxes with their unread counts, and Inbox, Sent, Spam, Trash and Calendar;
  - Blocked senders and Download a copy with a mailbox open, and Admin for controllers;
  - the storage used, Reload with the release, and Sign out.

  Escape, a tap beside it, or a choice closes it.
- **Search in the bar,** in Inbox, Spam and Trash. 🔍 opens the search field in place of the title. ✕ clears the search and closes the field.
- **Selection mode:**
  - A long press on a message, or ⋮ → Select messages, starts it. The checkboxes show only in this mode.
  - The bar then shows ✕, the number selected, and the folder's first two actions; its ⋮ holds Select all and the rest. In the Inbox that is Delete and Spam in the bar, and Mark read, Mark unread and Star in the ⋮. Spam and Trash have Not spam or Restore and Delete forever in the bar, and Star in the ⋮. Sent has Delete.
  - Mail still being sent cannot be selected.
- **An open message's toolbar is the bar:** ← Back, the subject, and the folder's actions. In the Inbox that is Reply and Delete in the bar, and Star, Spam and Mark unread in a ⋮. A sent message has Delete, and Send again where it applies, with no ⋮.
- **Compose floats** at the bottom right. Every page scrolls clear of it, and it hides while a compose window is open.
- **Rotating or resizing** past phone width closes the drawer and ends selection mode.

For maintainers:

- New: `webmail/src/AppBar.tsx` and `Drawer.tsx`, and `webmail/src/modal.ts`, the modal dialog hook shared with the calendar.
- Selection mode and the phone list bar are in `Bulk.tsx`.
- The header menu and the drawer share one list of account items. The side list and the drawer share one list of pages and one mailbox button.
- At phone width the desktop header is not rendered; at desktop width the phone pieces are not.
- Tests: 22 new Playwright tests at phone width (390x844), some of which also resize to desktop width. The tests that used the old phone folder tabs and mailbox select are updated.

## Upgrade notes

**Deploy only the webmail.** The backend is unchanged: this release's wasm is v0.12.2's, sha256 `462e5902a9147abb938e860f8db9c64a0df628a0684588d9311c4eb032f4798e`. So do not stop or upgrade the backend, and take no snapshot. The relay bridge is unchanged.

1. Pin v0.13.0 in the deployment repository.
2. Deploy the webmail, rebuilt with the deployment's brand. The release's unbranded `dist` hash is `a6469cf4056522977c9da165b6e80258c155b097f504960a45cb36f382da2078`; the branded output differs from it.
3. Run the deployment's checks, with the wasm sha256 above.
4. Reload the webmail on each phone. A phone may keep the old page until it is reloaded; the old page has Reload in its header's ⋮ menu.

This holds for a deployment on v0.12.2. A deployment on an earlier release first brings its backend and its webmail settings to v0.12.2, following the upgrade notes of each release after its own, including their checks and their changes to `webmail/public/.ic-assets.json5`. Then it deploys this webmail.

**Rolling back** means deploying v0.12.2's webmail again.

## Webmail and settings changes

- At phone width: the bar along the top, the ☰ drawer, search in the bar, selection mode, the open message's toolbar as the bar, and the floating Compose, as above.
- The desktop layout is unchanged.
- `webmail/public/.ic-assets.json5`, the `brand.mjs` options and the brand format are unchanged.
