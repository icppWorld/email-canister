# v0.3.0

## Overview

A mailbox-experience release, and the first that upgrades in place. Each mailbox gets Trash and Spam folders and a list of blocked senders. The webmail gets Delete with Undo, actions on hover and on several messages at once, stars in the list, Block sender, and a compose window that can be minimised or made full screen. At phone width the list and the open message take turns. Mailbox files are upgraded in place by a schema step that keeps the mail, so an existing v0.2.0 deployment is upgraded, not reinstalled.

## Highlights

- **Trash and Spam.** Each message is in the Inbox, Trash or Spam.
  - Delete moves mail to Trash with no confirmation, and leaves a note with Undo.
  - Trash has Restore, Delete forever and Empty Trash. Spam has Not spam, Delete forever and Empty Spam.
  - Mail in Trash or Spam for 30 days is deleted: up to 20 messages at a time, when new mail arrives in the mailbox and when mail is moved. It waits while the mailbox neither receives mail nor has mail moved.
  - The unread count counts the Inbox only. Search covers the folder shown.
- **Actions in the list.**
  - Each row has a checkbox and a star. On hover, Delete and Mark read or unread take the date's place: Restore and Delete forever in Trash and Spam.
  - Ticking messages shows a toolbar with Select all, the count, and the folder's actions. The webmail sends them 100 messages per call. A large selection goes in parts, and when a part is refused, what went through stays done and keeps its Undo.
- **Blocked senders.**
  - Block sender, in the reader, asks first. It then blocks the address for every member of the mailbox and moves the message to Spam, unless it is there already. A move that is refused leaves the block in place, with its Undo. Undo takes both back, unless the address was blocked already.
  - New mail from a blocked address is accepted and filed in Spam. Its envelope sender or its `From` address counts. Mail received before the block stays where it is, unless it was still waiting to be parsed.
  - The Blocked senders page lists each address with who blocked it and when, with Unblock. A mailbox blocks up to 1,000 addresses.
- **The folder stays after a send,** with a "Message sent" note and "View in Sent". A Sent list on screen reloads to show the message, also while older mail is shown.
- **A compose window** at the bottom right, with a title bar that shows the subject.
  - It can be minimised to a bar, keeping what it holds. A send refused while it is minimised opens it again, so the error shows.
  - It can be made full screen over a dimmed page.
  - At phone width it always fills the screen, and minimises to a bar along the bottom.
- **At phone width,** the list and the open message take turns, with a Back button in the reader, and the reader's actions wrap. On a touch screen, the row's checkbox and star and the compose window's buttons have targets of at least 44 px.
- **Notes with Undo** stay while the pointer is over them or the focus is in them. A note from an ended sign-in never shows in the next one.
- **Mailbox schema upgrades** keep the mail.
  - The ingest timer upgrades each mailbox file before it moves or parses mail into it. Meanwhile, the members' calls answer `busy:`. The webmail tries again a few times, then says the mailbox is being upgraded, and its next refresh tries again.
  - Upgrades take turns with incoming mail, so mailboxes already upgraded keep receiving while the others wait.
  - A step that fails holds its mailbox for repair only on its third failure in a row, a minute apart. A call the IC rejects before it runs does not count.
  - A file newer than the release, as after a rollback, keeps its version: nothing uses it, its members are told it needs a newer release, and `admin_check` says so without holding it.
  - Version 2, this release's step, adds the folders and the blocked senders. It adds two columns, a table and two indexes, without rewriting a row.
- **Tests:**
  - an upgrade test that installs the v0.2.0 release wasm, pinned by its sha256, delivers mail, upgrades to the build, and checks the mail, counters, search, Trash, Spam and blocking;
  - native and replica tests of the folder rules, and of an upgrade step that fails;
  - browser tests for each webmail action, and for every review fix.

## Candid API changes

Backend, `email-canister.did`:

- **`mail_list : (text, FolderRequest) -> (Page) query`**, with `FolderRequest = record { folder : nat64; cursor : opt nat64; limit : nat64 }`, in place of `ListRequest`. Folders are `0` Inbox, `1` Trash and `2` Spam; any other number traps.
- **`SearchRequest`** gains `folder : nat64`. A search covers that folder only.
- **`Summary`** gains `folder : nat64`.
- **`mail_set_flags : (text, vec nat64, nat64, nat64) -> ()`** takes up to 200 uids, in place of one.
- **`mail_delete : (text, vec nat64, nat64) -> (nat64)`** names the folder the messages are in, and deletes, for good, only those still there. A message that left the folder meanwhile, as when another member restored it, stays.
- **New:**
  - `mail_move : (text, vec nat64, nat64) -> (nat64)` moves up to 200 messages to a folder, and returns how many moved.
  - `mail_block : (text, text) -> ()` blocks an address for the mailbox. It traps when the mailbox blocks 1,000 addresses already.
  - `mail_unblock : (text, text) -> ()`.
  - `mail_blocked : (text) -> (vec Blocked) query`, with `Blocked = record { address : text; blocked_by : principal; name : opt text; at_ns : nat64 }`.
- `mail_outbox_list` keeps `ListRequest`.
- A mailbox held because its file is newer than the release answers its members that it needs a newer release.

Relay bridge: unchanged.

## Upgrade notes

**The backend is upgraded in place, and keeps its mail.** Upgrade the backend, then deploy the webmail: the new webmail calls the new methods, and the old webmail cannot list, flag or delete mail on the new backend. Users reload any webmail tab left open from before the deploy. The relay bridge is unchanged: nothing to copy into the Caffeine app.

1. Stop the backend and take a snapshot (`icp canister snapshot create email-canister --environment ic`).
2. Pin v0.3.0 in the deployment repository, whose `icp.yaml` installs the release's wasm only when its sha256 matches. Upgrade the backend (`icp canister install email-canister --mode upgrade --environment ic`), and start it.
3. Deploy the webmail. The brand format and `brand.mjs` options are unchanged.
4. Run the deployment's checks. The ingest timer upgrades each mailbox file to version 2, one file per step, taking turns with incoming mail, so a large deployment takes a few minutes. Until a mailbox's file is upgraded, its members' calls answer `busy:`, and the webmail says the mailbox is being upgraded. `admin_check` on each mailbox afterwards should answer `ok`.

**Rolling back** means restoring the snapshot, which puts the whole canister back as it was before the upgrade. Restore only when the upgrade cannot be fixed by a newer release, and know what it undoes:

- **Mail.** Mail received since the upgrade is lost. Members download their mailboxes from the webmail first.
- **Admin changes.** Every change made since the upgrade is undone: members removed, mailboxes disabled or purged, quotas, names, domain and bridge settings. A restored canister is stopped and answers nobody, admins included. So an admin writes a script beforehand that makes those changes again, and runs it the moment the canister is started. Until it has run, removed members can read and send from their old mailboxes, as they were at the snapshot.
- **Members' changes.** Mail deleted for good comes back, and blocks are as they were.
- **Sending.** Each mailbox's outbox counter goes back. For 7 days after a message was sent on v0.3.0, a new send can get its bridge key, and the relay bridge then answers with that message's result without sending anything. Mail that was waiting to be sent in the snapshot, and that v0.3.0 sent, is posted again: within those 7 days the bridge answers from its result, after them it sends the message a second time. A disabled mailbox posts its waiting mail only once it is enabled again, so the same holds then. So restore within 7 days of the upgrade, start the canister at once, and for 7 days have members confirm important mail by another way.
- **The webmail.** Deploy the v0.2.0 webmail again: the v0.3.0 webmail cannot list mail on the v0.2.0 backend.

Do not install v0.2.0 over upgraded files: v0.2.0 opens only version-1 files, so every upgraded mailbox would stop working until v0.3.0 is installed again.

## Webmail and settings changes

- Trash, Spam and Blocked senders in each mailbox's folder list, and routes `#<local>/trash`, `#<local>/spam` and `#<local>/blocked`.
- The list's checkboxes, stars and hover actions; the ticked messages' toolbar; the reader's Report spam, Block sender, Restore, Not spam and Delete forever.
- The note at the bottom left, with Undo or "View in Sent".
- The compose window's title bar with Minimise, Full screen and Close.
- At phone width: the list or the open message, with Back; compose fills the screen.
- The brand format and `brand.mjs` options are unchanged.

## Known issues

- At phone width, the device's back gesture leaves the webmail rather than returning from a message to the list. The reader's Back button returns.
- In full screen, the keyboard focus is not held inside the compose window.
- An Older mail click in Sent made while the list's first page reloads does nothing; click it again.
- A delete or move made in one browser shows in another member's list on its next refresh, within 20 seconds.
